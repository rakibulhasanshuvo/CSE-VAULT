# 🧮 Topic 11: Geometric Applications of Integration

This topic applies integration to find real-world spatial quantities: lengths of curves (rectification), areas of planar shapes, and volumes/surfaces of three-dimensional solids of revolution.

## 📑 Key Concepts
*   **Planar Area:** Area between curve $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ from $x=a$ to $x=b$:
    $$A = \int_a^b (f(x) - g(x)) \, dx$$
*   **Rectification (Arc Length $s$):** Finding the length of a curve $y = f(x)$ from $x=a$ to $x=b$:
    $$s = \int_a^b \sqrt{1 + \left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2} \, dx$$
*   **Volume of Solid of Revolution ($V$):** Revolving a curve $y=f(x)$ around the $x$-axis:
    *   *Disk Method:* $V = \int_a^b \pi [f(x)]^2 \, dx$
    *   *Shell Method (around y-axis):* $V = \int_a^b 2\pi x f(x) \, dx$
*   **Surface Area of Revolution ($S$):** Revolved around the $x$-axis:
    $$S = \int_a^b 2\pi y \sqrt{1 + \left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2} \, dx$$

## 🛠️ Practice These Problems
1.  Find the area bounded by the parabola $y = x^2$ and the line $y = 2x$.
2.  Find the arc length of the curve $y = \frac{2}{3}(x-1)^{3/2}$ from $x=1$ to $x=4$.
3.  Calculate the volume of the solid generated by revolving the region under $y = e^x$ from $x=0$ to $x=1$ around the $x$-axis.
4.  Find the surface area of a sphere of radius $r$ by revolving $y = \sqrt{r^2 - x^2}$ around the $x$-axis.
