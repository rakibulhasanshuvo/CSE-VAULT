## 📑 Key Formulas
*   **Power Rule:** `d/dx [xⁿ] = nxⁿ⁻¹`
*   **Product Rule:** `(uv)' = u'v + uv'`
*   **Quotient Rule:** `(u/v)' = (u'v - uv') / v²`
*   **Tangent Line Equation:** `y - y₁ = m(x - x₁)` where `m = f'(x₁)`.

## **Topic 4: Geometric Derivatives, Tangents, and the Chain Rule**

### **Theoretical Framework and Rules**

The derivative ![](../assets/image131.png) serves as the mathematical realization of instantaneous geometry, mapping the localized slope of the tangent bundle on a manifold. However, complex mathematical formulations rarely exist as isolated variables; they are often nested within layers of functions. The Chain Rule establishes the rigorous protocol for differentiating these composite structures, dictating that ![](../assets/image132.png).21 This implies that the instantaneous rate of change of a composed function is the multiplication of the outer layer's rate of change (relative to the inner layer) by the inner layer's rate of change (relative to the base base variable).21 The cascading effect of the chain rule is instrumental in physics, creating formal relationships between varied kinematic reference frames—for instance, modeling how temporal changes dynamically propagate through a fluid's spatial distortion.
