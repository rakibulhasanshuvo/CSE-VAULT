# 📈 7-Day Intensive Study Plan for Calculus & Vectors

Since you have an exam next week and a study gap to bridge, this plan focuses on the most common exam questions: Limits, Differentiation rules, and Max/Min applications.

## 📅 Day 1: Number Systems & Functions (Week 1)
**Topics:** Number system, Function illustration, Domains and Ranges
*   **Focus:** Refresh your understanding of Real numbers, Integers, and Rational numbers. Master how to find the **Domain** and **Range** of a function (especially fractions and square roots).
*   **Action Item:** Practice identifying function types (linear, quadratic, cubic, reciprocal).

## 📅 Day 2: Graphing & Parametric Equations (Week 2)
**Topics:** Drawing graphs, Parametric equations
*   **Focus:** Review how to shift and reflect graphs. Understand **Parametric Equations** (where x and y are defined by a third variable *t*).
*   **Action Item:** Convert a few parametric equations into standard Cartesian (x,y) equations and sketch them.

## 📅 Day 3: Limits & L'Hospital's Rule (Week 3)
**Topics:** State Limit, L'Hospital’s rule
*   **Focus:** This is a high-probability exam topic. Master the indeterminate forms (0/0 and ∞/∞). Learn when and how to apply **L'Hospital's Rule** (taking the derivative of the top and bottom).
*   **Action Item:** Solve 10 limit problems, at least 5 using L'Hospital's Rule.

## 📅 Day 4: Continuity & Differentiation (Week 4)
**Topics:** Interpretation of continuity, Calculating differentiation, Tangent lines
*   **Focus:** Understand the 3 conditions for a function to be continuous. Review the power rule, product rule, and quotient rule for differentiation. Learn how to find the **Equation of a Tangent Line** at a specific point.
*   **Action Item:** Practice finding the derivative of complex fractions using the Quotient Rule.

## 📅 Day 5: Applications of Differentiation (Week 5)
**Topics:** Successive differentiation, Maximum and minimum problems
*   **Focus:** Learn the Second Derivative (successive differentiation). Focus heavily on **Optimization Problems** (finding the Max/Min values in word problems).
*   **Action Item:** Solve "Critical Point" problems where you set the first derivative to zero.

## 📅 Day 6: Review Part 1 (Basics & Limits)
*   **Morning:** Review Day 1-3 formulas. Create a "Limits Cheat Sheet" with common limit identities.
*   **Afternoon:** Solve past paper questions on Limits and Parametric equations.
*   **Evening:** Re-watch tutorials for any concept that still feels "gappy."

## 📅 Day 7: Review Part 2 (Calculus Applications)
*   **Morning:** Review differentiation rules and the Tangent Line formula.
*   **Afternoon:** Solve 5-10 Max/Min word problems. These are usually the highest-mark questions.
*   **Evening:** Quick final review of your formula sheet and get plenty of rest.

---
> **💡 Pro Tip:** Calculus is a "ladder" subject. If you don't understand Day 4 (Differentiation), Day 5 (Max/Min) will be impossible. Don't skip ahead!
