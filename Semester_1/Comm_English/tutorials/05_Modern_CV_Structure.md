# 📄 Topic 5: Modern CV Structure

Your CV is your "sales pitch" to a future employer.

## 📑 Standard CV Layout
1.  **Contact Information:** Name, Phone, Email, LinkedIn (No photo needed in some countries).
2.  **Professional Summary:** 2-3 sentences about who you are.
3.  **Education:** Start with your **most recent** degree. Include dates and GPA.
4.  **Work Experience:** Start with your current/last job. Use "Action Verbs" (Managed, Developed, Created).
5.  **Skills:** Technical skills (Python, Excel) and Soft skills (Leadership, Communication).
6.  **References:** "Available upon request" or list 2-3 professional names.

## 🛠️ Exam Tip
If the exam asks you to "Write a CV for an Engineering Intern position," make sure you highlight **relevant coursework** and **projects** since you might not have job experience yet.

---
> **💡 Pro Tip:** Keep your CV to **1-2 pages maximum**. Use a clean font like Arial or Calibri. Consistency in formatting (bolding, dates) is key to a professional look.
