# 🧩 Topic 3: Paragraph Unity & Transitions

Making your writing flow like a river, not a series of puddles.

## 📑 Structure of a Paragraph
1.  **Topic Sentence:** Tells the reader what the paragraph is about.
2.  **Supporting Sentences:** Provides evidence, examples, or explanation.
3.  **Concluding/Transition Sentence:** Wraps up the point and links to the next paragraph.

## 🔗 Transition Words (Memorize These!)
*   **To Add Information:** Furthermore, Moreover, In addition.
*   **To Show Contrast:** However, Conversely, On the other hand.
*   **To Show Result:** Therefore, Consequently, As a result.
*   **To Summarize:** In conclusion, To sum up, Overall.

> **💡 Pro Tip:** **Linguistic Unity** means every sentence in the paragraph must support the **Topic Sentence**. If a sentence starts talking about something else, move it to a new paragraph!
