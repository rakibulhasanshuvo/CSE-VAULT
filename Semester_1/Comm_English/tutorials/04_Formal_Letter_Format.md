# ✉️ Topic 4: Formal Letter Format

The "Block Format" is the most common and safest format for exams.

## 📑 Layout Checklist (In Order)
1.  **Sender’s Address:** (Top left or top right).
2.  **Date:** (e.g., May 15, 2024).
3.  **Inside Address:** (Receiver's name/title and company address).
4.  **Salutation:** (Dear Mr. Smith / Dear Sir or Madam).
5.  **Subject Line:** (Briefly state the purpose, e.g., SUB: Application for Sick Leave).
6.  **The Body:** (Introduction, Purpose, Detailed Request, Conclusion).
7.  **Closing:** (Sincerely / Yours faithfully).
8.  **Signature & Name.**

## 🛠️ Key Tips
*   **Formal:** Use "Yours faithfully" if you don't know the name; "Yours sincerely" if you do.
*   **Informal:** Use "Best regards" or "Love" (for family).

---
> **💡 Pro Tip:** In a formal letter, **never use contractions** (use "do not" instead of "don't"). It shows respect and professionalism.
