# 🗣️ Topic 1: Speech vs. Writing

Understanding the two primary modes of communication.

## 📑 Key Differences
| Feature | Speech | Writing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | Transient (Gone once spoken) | Permanent (Can be re-read) |
| **Structure** | Often fragmented/informal | Grammatically structured |
| **Feedback** | Immediate feedback from listener | Delayed or no feedback |
| **Aids** | Uses tone, body language, pitch | Uses punctuation, layout, fonts |
| **Precision** | Less precise, more repetition | Highly precise and concise |

## 🛠️ Exam Practice
> "Explain why written communication is preferred for official records while speech is preferred for conflict resolution."
*   *Hint:* Discuss **Permanence** for records and **Immediate Feedback/Tone** for resolution.

---
> **💡 Pro Tip:** Remember the term **"Paralinguistic Features"** for speech (body language, facial expressions) and **"Graphological Features"** for writing (punctuation, italics). Use these terms in your exam!
