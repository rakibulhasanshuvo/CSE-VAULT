# ✍️ 7-Day Intensive Study Plan for Comm. English

This subject is all about **Format** and **Clarity**. If you know the structure, you get the marks.

## 📅 Day 1: Communication Basics (Week 1)
**Topics:** Language and Communication; Speech vs. Writing
*   **Focus:** Understand the differences between oral and written communication. Speech is spontaneous; Writing is permanent and structured.
*   **Action Item:** List 5 features of speech and 5 features of writing.

## 📅 Day 2: The Writing Process (Week 2)
**Topics:** Topic selection, thesis statement, and development
*   **Focus:** How to start an essay. Focus heavily on the **Thesis Statement** (the main argument).
*   **Action Item:** Practice writing a thesis statement for 3 different topics (e.g., "The impact of social media").

## 📅 Day 3: Paragraph Structure (Week 3)
**Topics:** Introductory, Transitional, and Concluding paragraphs
*   **Focus:** Every paragraph needs a **Topic Sentence**. Focus on **Transition Words** (However, Therefore, Furthermore) to ensure linguistic unity.
*   **Action Item:** Write one perfect introductory paragraph for an essay.

## 📅 Day 4: Precise & Letter Writing (Week 4)
**Topics:** Precise writing; Letter writing (Personal, Formal, Semi-formal)
*   **Focus:** **Precise writing** (summarizing) means cutting 1/3rd of the text while keeping the meaning. **Letter writing** requires mastering the "Formal" block format.
*   **Action Item:** Practice 1 formal letter to a manager and 1 informal letter to a friend.

## 📅 Day 5: CV & Resume Writing (Week 5)
**Topics:** CV/Resume writing
*   **Focus:** The structure of a modern CV. Contact info → Summary → Experience → Education → Skills.
*   **Action Item:** Draft your own professional CV using the standard academic format.

## 📅 Day 6: Review Part 1 (Essay & Paragraphs)
*   **Morning:** Review Thesis statements and Paragraph transitions.
*   **Afternoon:** Practice 2-3 "Precise writing" exercises.
*   **Evening:** Quick review of Speech vs. Writing features.

## 📅 Day 7: Review Part 2 (Letters & CVs)
*   **Morning:** Memorize the layout of a Formal Letter (Sender address, Date, Receiver address, Salutation, Body, Closing).
*   **Afternoon:** Review the CV structure.
*   **Evening:** Final check of common grammar and punctuation rules.

---
> **💡 Pro Tip:** In English exams, **Presentation Matters**. Use clear headings, bullet points in CVs, and proper indentation in letters to make your paper easy for the examiner to read!
