# 🌍 7-Day Intensive Study Plan for Technology & Society

This subject focuses on the interaction between human society and technological progress. Since your exam is next week, focus on defining key terms and understanding social theories.

## 📅 Day 1: Definitions & Uses (Week 1)
**Topics:** Society, science and technology: Definition and uses
*   **Focus:** Understand the basic definitions of Science vs. Technology. How does technology "drive" society (Technological Determinism)?
*   **Action Item:** Write down 3 ways technology has changed your local society in the last 10 years.

## 📅 Day 2: Social Research Methods (Week 2)
**Topics:** Basic concepts, methods, and stages of social research
*   **Focus:** Understand how sociologists study people. Difference between Qualitative (interviews) and Quantitative (surveys) research.
*   **Action Item:** Memorize the 5-7 stages of a social research project (Topic selection → Data collection → Analysis).

## 📅 Day 3: Culture & Cultural Lag (Week 3)
**Topics:** Culture and civilization: cultural lag
*   **Focus:** Define Culture vs. Civilization. Master the concept of **"Cultural Lag"** (when technology advances faster than our laws or morals).
*   **Action Item:** Find a modern example of cultural lag (e.g., AI and Copyright laws).

## 📅 Day 4: Socialization & Personality (Week 4)
**Topics:** Socialization and personality development
*   **Focus:** How do we become "socialized"? Primary vs. Secondary socialization. The role of family, peers, and media.
*   **Action Item:** Define "Nature vs. Nurture" and how it relates to personality.

## 📅 Day 5: Stratification & Inequality (Week 5)
**Topics:** Social stratification and social inequality
*   **Focus:** Why are societies divided into "layers"? Class, Status, and Power. How does technology bridge or widen the gap (The Digital Divide)?
*   **Action Item:** Learn the difference between "Open" vs. "Closed" stratification systems.

## 📅 Day 6: Review Part 1 (Weeks 1-3)
*   **Morning:** Review definitions of Technology, Research Methods, and Culture.
*   **Afternoon:** Practice writing short essays on how technology affects culture.
*   **Evening:** Quick review of "Cultural Lag" examples.

## 📅 Day 7: Review Part 2 (Weeks 4-5)
*   **Morning:** Review Socialization agents and Stratification theories.
*   **Afternoon:** Focus on "The Digital Divide" as it connects technology to inequality.
*   **Evening:** Final read-through of all key sociological terms and definitions.

---
> **💡 Pro Tip:** In this subject, examiners love **real-world examples**. When you define a term like "Cultural Lag," always provide a current example (like AI or CRISPR) to get full marks!
