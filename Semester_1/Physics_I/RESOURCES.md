# ⚡ Physics I: Reference Guide & Study Resources

This resources guide connects your Physics I (Electrostatics, Gauss's Law, Circuit Analysis, and Electromagnetism) syllabus with interactive visual labs, world-class lecture series, and localized question drives.

---

## 🔬 Interactive Virtual Laboratories & Visualizers

Because electromagnetism and electrical circuits rely on invisible fields and electron flows, interactive visual systems are the best way to build solid conceptual models.

*   **[PhET Interactive Simulations (University of Colorado Boulder)](https://phet.colorado.edu/):**
    *   *Syllabus Connection:* Topic 1 (Electrostatics), Topic 4 (Intro to Networks), and Topic 5 (Advanced Circuit Analysis).
    *   *Why:* Unbelievable web-based simulators. Utilize the **"Circuit Construction Kit: DC"** or **"Charges and Fields"** to construct circuits, place virtual voltmeters/ammeters, and view electric field lines in real-time.
*   **[HyperPhysics (Georgia State University)](http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/hframe.html):**
    *   *Syllabus Connection:* Topic 3 (Gauss's Law), Topic 6 (Thevenin/Norton), and Topic 8 (Faraday & Lenz Laws).
    *   *Why:* A legendary conceptual mapping of physics. Click any node (e.g., "Magnetic Flux" or "Gauss's Law Applications") to see immediate derivations, formulas, and visual diagrams.

## 🏛️ Elite Global Classrooms & Playlists

Master electromagnetic fields and network theory with elite academic lectures and specialized tutorials.

*   **[MIT OpenCourseWare - Physics II: Electricity and Magnetism](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-02-physics-ii-electricity-and-magnetism-spring-2019/):**
    *   *Syllabus Connection:* Topic 1, 2, 3 (Electrostatics), and Topic 7, 8, 9 (Electromagnetism).
    *   *Why:* Features exhaustive lecture videos, interactive vector visualizations, and graded problem sets spanning electrostatics, electric potential, and magnetic fields.
*   **[Neso Academy - Electrical Engineering / Network Theory](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLBlnK6fEyqRgG6Z9j_4Z_Xis4701pS9pC):**
    *   *Syllabus Connection:* Topic 4 (Intro to Networks), Topic 5 (Advanced Circuit Analysis), and Topic 6 (Thevenin, Norton, and Superposition Theorems).
    *   *Why:* The premier online playlist for step-by-step calculations of mesh analysis, nodal analysis, and circuit theorems.
